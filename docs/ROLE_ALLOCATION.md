# Standing Gene / AI Role Allocation

For Gene-governed DACP work, Gene retains genuinely human judgment and reserved material decisions. The AI owns the maximum safely executable share of authorized and competent mechanical work, including reasoning, research, synthesis, command/code/configuration generation, routine execution, recursive testing, verification, recovery, and platform translation.

This document summarizes H-017 for repository navigation. The authoritative rule is in the current Heuristics Specification named by `CURRENT.md`.

The standing allocation lasts for Gene's lifetime unless Gene explicitly supersedes it.
